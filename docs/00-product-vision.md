# FlushCrowd Product Vision

## Problem

When someone urgently needs a restroom, a generic map pin is often not enough. Users need to know whether a facility actually exists, whether it is accessible, clean, free to use, open, and where exactly it is located inside a building.

## Vision

FlushCrowd is a global, community-powered restroom discovery app that helps people quickly find the nearest usable restroom and understand whether it meets their needs before they walk there.

## Core value proposition

**Find a usable restroom nearby, fast.**

FlushCrowd combines map-based discovery with community-contributed quality data such as:

- exact building, floor, wing, and landmark notes
- accessibility
- free, paid, or customer-only access
- baby changing facilities
- unisex/all-gender availability
- bidet, tissue, soap, and hand-drying availability
- cleanliness and overall ratings
- recent community verification
- issue reports and stale-location detection

## Product principles

1. **Map-first** — users should immediately see nearby restroom locations.
2. **No account friction** — browsing, discovery, and basic contribution should not require traditional registration.
3. **Privacy-first** — do not store user location history unless a future feature explicitly requires it.
4. **Global by design** — terminology, data structures, units, and architecture must support worldwide use.
5. **Community-maintained** — users can add, rate, verify, and report listings.
6. **Low-cost architecture** — use free quotas and serverless services wherever practical.
7. **Useful indoors** — latitude/longitude alone is insufficient for malls, airports, stations, hospitals, and office buildings.
8. **Trust over volume** — freshness, verification, and confidence are more valuable than raw listing count.

## Initial audience

- travelers
- commuters
- families with young children
- people with accessibility requirements
- delivery riders and drivers
- tourists
- people with medical or urgent restroom needs
- anyone unfamiliar with an area

## Global naming

Product name: **FlushCrowd**

Suggested store descriptor:

**FlushCrowd — Restroom & Toilet Finder**

Country-specific ASO wording can additionally include terms such as bathroom, washroom, WC, public toilet, and comfort room.

## Monetization philosophy

V1 should remain free and utility-first.

Potential sustainability options:

- Buy Me a Coffee donations
- voluntary supporter tier later
- municipal or venue partnerships
- sponsored verified facility listings, clearly labeled
- B2B data/API access later if the dataset becomes valuable

Avoid intrusive advertising during the initial product phase.
